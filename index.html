    Before your first session

    I've never played. Is that a problem?

    No. First Rally is built for people who have never held a padel racket. Most players can keep a rally going by the end of the first hour. We lend rackets, and if you play tennis or squash you'll progress faster.

    What should I wear?

    Light sports clothes and court shoes with a herringbone or omni sole. Running shoes slip on the sand-filled turf and are the most common cause of rolled ankles.

    What happens when it rains?

    Nothing. Every court we use is covered or indoor, so the monsoon months from May to October don't cancel sessions.

    Isn't it too hot?

    We run sessions at 07:00 and from 17:00 to 22:30 to avoid the midday heat. Cold water and towels are at every court.

    Can I come on my own?

    Yes. Pick a Clinic slot and we'll place you with three players at your level.
