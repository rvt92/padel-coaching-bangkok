    Playing padel in Bangkok: questions from visitors

    What is padel?

    Padel is a doubles racket sport played on a court of 10 × 20 m enclosed by glass walls. You use a solid racket without strings, serve underarm and can play the ball off the walls. It's easier to pick up than tennis, so most beginners are rallying within their first hour.

    Can I play padel in Bangkok as a visitor?

    Yes. Pick a group class, clinic or americano from the timetable, then message us with the session and your dates. You can also book private coaching with any of the coaches.

    Do I need a partner?

    No. Group classes, clinics and americanos are grouped by level, so you can come on your own.

    I've never played. Where do I start?

    Choose a Starter Class or Padel 101, or a Beginner Americano if you'd rather play straight away. The level finder on this page gives you a starting point.

    Do I need to bring a racket?

    No. You can rent a racket at the club for ฿150 per session. Just mention it when you book.

    Where are the clubs?

    KROSS has four main clubs on or near Sukhumvit Road: On Nut, Asoke, Sky Club (Sukhumvit 49) and Indoor (Khlong Toei). Each one has a Google Maps link above.

    When is the best time to play in Bangkok?

    Early mornings and evenings are the coolest times. On very hot or rainy days, KROSS Indoor is the most comfortable choice.

    What should I wear?

    Light sports clothes and court shoes with a herringbone or omni sole. Running shoes slip on the sand-filled turf and are the most common cause of rolled ankles.

    How accurate is the level finder?

    It's an estimate on the 0–7 scale used by most padel booking apps. Your coach confirms your level in your first session.
